About

Hi, I'm Katherine.

I'm an English and special education teacher in the Rochester area. Over the past two years I've taught across the grade span, from kindergarten through twelfth grade: full ELA units in a high school, small-group reading support, and two self-contained 12:1:1 rooms with younger students.

I finished my B.S. in English Adolescence Inclusive Education at SUNY Brockport in December 2025, magna cum laude, and I'm working on my master's now. My New York State certification exams in English (7–12), the 5–6 extension, and students with disabilities across all grades are scheduled for summer 2026.

The part I care about most is about building meaningful relationships with students. When a student trusts that the room is safe, they'll expand their comfort zone, ask thoughtful questions, and work through difficult problems. That's the environment I strive to promote.
